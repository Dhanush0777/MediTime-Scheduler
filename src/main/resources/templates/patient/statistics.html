How Adherence is Calculated

Adherence percentage reflects the ratio of successfully taken doses against total scheduled medication doses:
Adherence % = (Taken Doses / Total Scheduled Doses) × 100
Maintaining high adherence (>80%) ensures optimal therapeutic efficacy and prevents disease recurrence.
